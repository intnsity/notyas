# Device photographs

Photographs of notyas running on real hardware, taken 2026-08-17 on the 0.1.0-m4 build.
The rendered UI images under `docs/screenshots/ui/` are host-generated by `tools/uisim`
and are the colour reference; these are physical-device shots for the hardware sections.

## Colour note - read this before "fixing" anything

In the two photographs that show screen content, the Butter Paper background looks pale
cyan. **The devices are correct.** Viewed directly, the panels show the intended warm
butter-paper yellow, confirmed by the device owner.

The blue cast is a camera artefact: auto white balance metered for the warm room light,
and an emissive LCD photographed under those conditions renders cold. Reflective surfaces
in the same frame (the desk, the dice) keep their warm tone, which makes the effect look
more convincing than it is.

A colour "bug" was diagnosed from these photographs and was wrong. Do not adjust the
theme constants, the `DrawTarget` byte order, or the panel colour-element order on
photographic evidence. If on-device colour is ever genuinely in question, render a test
card of known patches and compare it against `docs/screenshots/ui/02-dice-entry.png` by
eye on the panel itself.
