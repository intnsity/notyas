## S-04 PIN entry

- Submit disabled reason: "A PIN is at least 6 characters."
- Below 6 characters: Unlock disabled.

The entry surface accepts full alphanumeric (OPEN-QUESTIONS Q4, ratified: minimum 6
characters, no maximum below 64).
